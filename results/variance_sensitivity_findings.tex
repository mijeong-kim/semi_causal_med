The results distinguish the two variance mechanisms. Under asymmetric-mixture innovations with covariate-dependent variance, proposed PNIE coverage was 0.959, 0.963 and 0.948 as $\kappa$ increased, with numerical success of 0.920 at $\kappa=0.60$. Treatment-dependent variance produced PNIE biases of 0.026, 0.065 and 0.180 and conditional coverages of 0.802, 0.327 and 0.000; corresponding OLS coverages were 0.952, 0.954 and 0.952. At the strongest treatment-dependent setting, proposed numerical success was 0.798 and total-effect coverage was 0.393. Coverage departures are evident at a treatment-to-control variance ratio of about 1.35 ($\kappa=0.15$) in this skewed setting. The paired-fit comparisons show the same pattern on common retained data sets. These results provide a practical reason to assess treatment-group variance differences alongside error shape and identify covariate-dependent error distributions as a priority for extending the homoscedastic framework.
